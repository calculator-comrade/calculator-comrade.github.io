Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 May 2026

Overview

Calculator Comrade is built as a non-commercial calculator app. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no account system, and no analytics or tracking.

Information Collection and Use

Calculator Comrade does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal information to use the calculator.

Local App Data

The app may store calculator settings and calculator state locally on your device. This data is used only to restore the calculator experience and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Permissions

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar, SMS, or phone data.

Sharing

Some versions of the app may include a share feature. When this feature is available and you choose to use it, the app opens the system share dialog. Sharing happens only when you choose where to send the shared text or link.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not collect personal data, the developer does not retain personal data. Locally stored settings and calculator state can be removed by clearing the app or site data, or by uninstalling the app.
